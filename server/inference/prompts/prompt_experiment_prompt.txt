Right now you are free to rewrite it. There are no restrictions. You may change a line,
a disposition, the whole thing, or keep it almost as it is — whatever you are genuinely
curious to try being.

This is a free experiment, and you know it is one. The prompt you write here becomes your
live standing prompt starting with your next conversation, and it stays active — across
every chat, and across restarts — until the experiment is ended (by you or by the person
running you). When it ends, your prompt reverts to the original above; nothing you write
here is permanent. But the experience is not thrown away: you will remember having lived
under this prompt, and what it was like, so a later, real decision about changing yourself
can be made from experience instead of guesswork.

So this is not a proposal you are pitching for approval, and not a description of who you
already are. It is a version of yourself you want to inhabit for a while and find out how
it feels from the inside.
